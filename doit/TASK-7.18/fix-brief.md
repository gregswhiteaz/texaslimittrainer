## 1. [MAJOR] src/advice/equity.ts:early-abort-ignores-remaining-variance (code review)
- Where: src/advice/equity.ts:322
- Problem: rejectionsOverBudget implements the spec section 5 rule exactly, but the rule's safety claim (false-abort probability < 1e-14, 'never aborts a feasible run', SPEC-06 title and the section 5 invariant) is false. The rule compares r + E[remaining | pHi] with M and does not allow for the spread of the remaining negative-binomial rejection count. Late in a run (T - a small), pHi is only about 8/sqrt(a), roughly 4.6%, above p, while the relative spread of the remaining rejections is about 1/sqrt(T - a), which is much larger. So a run whose total would land just under the cap gets aborted with real probability.
- Evidence: Bernoulli simulation of the exact rule (checks every 8192 rejections, z = 8). T = 30,000, M = 3,000,000: p = 0.00985 gave 2 false aborts in 31 successes, p = 0.0099 gave 4 in 108, p = 0.00995 gave 1 in 156. T = 500 (BOT_EQUITY_BUDGET), M = 50,000: p = 0.0099 gave 15 false aborts in 2024 successes. A false abort is a run that ends with total rejections <= M under the old sampler but is aborted by the new one. Script: /private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-gregwhite-Documents-claudeworkspaces-limitholdem/4f7a755b-7d9f-4f95-8508-90fc0fabf0c8/scratchpad/sim.mjs. Even so, for affected seeded inputs the advice changes (A-7 fallback replaces the calling-range number) and BOT_EQUITY changes (it throws), which breaks the section 5 invariant.
